What can you do with ReviewGuard AI?

You can enter a product review and the system analyses it using Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Machine Learning to classify the review as potentially fake or likely genuine .

You can also upload a CSV file containing multiple reviews and analyse them together using Batch CSV Analysis .

The system also provides a confidence score for each prediction and the results can be viewed through the Dashboard .
